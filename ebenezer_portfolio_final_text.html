About me

I build, learn and improve.

I enjoy web development because it gives me a practical way to turn ideas into working products.

I care about building simple, useful and well-made websites.

My main focus is web development. I work on both the frontend and backend, and I enjoy understanding how the different parts of a web application fit together.

I like clean layouts, straightforward user experiences and code that is easy to understand and maintain. Most of what I know has come from a mix of school, personal projects, practice and building things for real people.

Cybersecurity is also part of my background, so I try to keep security in mind when I build. It is not the main focus of this portfolio, but it is something I bring into the way I approach development.
